Our Evolution

Phase I - Ideation

The Concept of Calm Computing

Recognizing the need for a non-intrusive academic workspace, initial prototypes were developed focusing purely on typography, spatial rhythm, and reducing structural UI elements.

Phase II - Architecture

Integrating the AI Tutor

Development of the contextual engine. The goal was to ensure the AI acts as a sounding board rather than a blunt generation tool, maintaining academic integrity.

Phase III - Current

Refining the Canvas

Continuous iteration on the glassmorphism aesthetic and performance optimization to handle large-scale document synthesis without breaking the minimalist illusion.
