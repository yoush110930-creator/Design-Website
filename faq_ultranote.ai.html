AI Features & Synthesis

How does the AI Tutor synthesize large PDFs? expand_more
Ultra.note utilizes a multi-stage semantic chunking process. When you upload a PDF, the AI Tutor first indexes the document structure, then applies our proprietary context-window management to cross-reference concepts across hundreds of pages without losing specific citations.
Are my academic notes used to train the global model? expand_more
No. Your intellectual property is protected. All document processing is compartmentalized, and we operate under a strict zero-retention policy for training data. Your notes remain entirely local to your workspace ecosystem.
Can I adjust the depth of the AI summaries? expand_more
Yes. In the AI Tutor settings panel, you can adjust the "Synthesis Granularity" slider. Move it towards "Abstract" for high-level overviews, or "Granular" to extract specific methodologies, data points, and inline citations.

Account & Workspace

How do I export my entire knowledge base? expand_more
Navigate to Settings > Workspace > Data Management. You can export your entire library in standard formats including Markdown, JSON, and PDF. The export includes all your metadata, tags, and AI-generated insight links.
Does Ultra.note support offline mode? expand_more
Basic note-taking and viewing cached documents are fully supported offline. However, semantic search and generative AI features require an active connection to interface with the inference engines. Changes made offline will sync automatically upon reconnection.

Privacy & Security

We design our architecture with academic integrity and institutional data protection standards as foundational principles.

Is my data encrypted at rest? expand_more
Yes. All databases and document storage buckets are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. We utilize industry-standard key management services to ensure secure access control.
Can I enforce SSO for my research team? expand_more
Single Sign-On (SSO) integration via SAML or OIDC is available on Enterprise and Institutional tiers. Contact our enterprise support team to configure SSO for your organization's domain.
